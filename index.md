# Muxivo

**Plays the files your Mac won't — and puts them on your TV, with picture.**

Muxivo opens MKV, AVI, TS and the rest, then streams them to any AirPlay
television with video *and* audio. Other players can send the sound and leave
the picture on your device; Muxivo sends the film.

Available for the **Mac**. There is no iPhone or iPad app yet, and no app that
runs on the Apple TV itself — those are [being worked on](roadmap).

- **MKV, MP4, AVI, TS/M2TS, VOB** — including high-bitrate HD video
- **Subtitles that work** — SRT and embedded text subtitles
- **Surround sound** — AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, and multichannel AAC and FLAC
- **Starts in seconds** — you don't wait for a conversion to finish
- **Tells you the truth** — which audio track is playing, and why
