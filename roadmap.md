# Roadmap

What is being worked on, and what it is meant to fix. Some of it is written and
measured, some has not been started; none of it has shipped yet.

## Starting faster

Most films start straight away, but some take longer than they should before the
first picture appears. That fix is written and measured on a test build. The
first opening of a very large film still takes a few seconds, and shortening
that has not been started.

## Subtitles

Some subtitles look plainer than they should — the words and their position are
right, the styling is not. Others are listed in the menu and then never appear
once you choose them. The work to draw them properly is written and looks right
in testing, but nothing in the app can reach it yet, and making the listed ones
appear is part of the same job.

## The interface

Choosing the next video from your library ends the cast, so watching a series
means reconnecting for every episode. That is the next thing being worked on.

## iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

Muxivo runs on the Mac. An iPhone and iPad version, and an app that runs on the
Apple TV itself, are both being worked on. Neither is finished, and there is
nothing honest to say yet about which arrives first.

## More televisions

Muxivo sends video to AirPlay televisions and only those. Support for other
televisions is being explored, and that work has a long way to go.

---

This is not a schedule or a commitment. Priorities move. If the thing that gets
in your way is not here, [tell us](support) — it is how the order gets decided.
