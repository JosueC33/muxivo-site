Plays the files your Mac won't — and puts them on your TV, with picture

The problem it solves

Ever streamed to your TV and only got the sound?

Other players

  • Draw the video frames themselves, so they can hand AirPlay only the audio
  • The sound goes to the TV; the picture stays on your device

Muxivo

  • Sends the film: picture and sound
  • Converts on your Mac in real time — nothing is uploaded anywhere
  • Keep using other apps while the TV plays
2 / 7
Play everything

The files Apple's own players refuse

  • MKV, MP4, MOV, AVI, TS/M2TS, VOB — including high-bitrate HD video
  • HEVC/H.265 and H.264, 4K and HDR sources
  • Surround sound — AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, and multichannel AAC and FLAC — with multiple audio tracks
  • Subtitles that work — SRT and embedded text subtitles, plus Blu-ray picture subtitles on the Mac
  • Starts in seconds — you don't wait for a conversion to finish
  • Tells you the truth — which audio track is playing, and why
