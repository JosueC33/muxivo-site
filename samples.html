How to open them

No account or server required

Mac Download a file, then open Muxivo and choose File ▸ Open, or drag the file onto the window. No account or server is required for local playback.
iPhone and iPad Coming soon Those versions are coming soon — see the roadmap.
On a television Start playback and use the AirPlay control in the player. Any Apple TV or other AirPlay-compatible television works as the receiver.
